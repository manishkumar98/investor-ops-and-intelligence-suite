Fund Overview:
SBI Focused Equity Fund aims to provide investors with opportunities for long-term growth in capital by investing in a concentrated portfolio of equity and equity-related securities of maximum 30 stocks across market capitalisations.

The fund invests in a focused portfolio of up to 30 high-conviction stocks across large-cap, mid-cap, and small-cap companies.

The fund follows a blend of growth and value style of investing with a bottom-up approach for stock selection.

FAQ Section:
What is SBI Focused Equity Fund?
SBI Focused Equity Fund is an open-ended equity scheme that invests in a concentrated portfolio of maximum 30 stocks across market capitalisations (large, mid, and small cap). It is suitable for investors seeking long-term capital appreciation with a high-conviction, focused approach.

What is the exit load of SBI Focused Equity Fund?
The exit load is 1% if units are redeemed within 1 year from the date of allotment. There is no exit load after 1 year.

What is the expense ratio of SBI Focused Equity Fund?
The expense ratio for the Direct Plan is 0.84% and for the Regular Plan is 1.80%.

What is the minimum SIP amount for SBI Focused Equity Fund?
The minimum SIP amount is ₹500.

Is there a lock-in period for SBI Focused Equity Fund?
No, there is no mandatory lock-in period for SBI Focused Equity Fund.

What is the benchmark for SBI Focused Equity Fund?
The scheme benchmark is BSE 500 TRI and the additional benchmark is BSE Sensex TRI.
