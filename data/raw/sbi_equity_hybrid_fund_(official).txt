Fund Overview:
SBI Equity Hybrid Fund is an open-ended aggressive hybrid scheme that aims to provide investors with long-term capital appreciation and current income by investing in a mix of equity and fixed income instruments.

The fund invests 65–80% of its assets in equity and equity-related instruments and 20–35% in debt and money market instruments. This balanced allocation provides growth potential from equities while the debt component offers stability and income.

The equity portion follows a diversified approach across large-cap, mid-cap, and small-cap companies. The debt portion invests in high-quality bonds, government securities, and money market instruments.

FAQ Section:
What is SBI Equity Hybrid Fund?
SBI Equity Hybrid Fund is an aggressive hybrid mutual fund that invests 65–80% in equities and 20–35% in debt instruments. It is suitable for investors who want equity-driven growth with some stability from debt allocation.

What is the exit load of SBI Equity Hybrid Fund?
The exit load is 1% if units are redeemed within 1 year from the date of allotment. There is no exit load after 1 year.

What is the expense ratio of SBI Equity Hybrid Fund?
The expense ratio for the Direct Plan is 0.84% and for the Regular Plan is 1.62%.

What is the minimum SIP amount?
The minimum SIP amount is ₹500.

What is the minimum lumpsum investment?
The minimum lumpsum investment is ₹1,000.

Is there a lock-in period?
No, there is no mandatory lock-in period for SBI Equity Hybrid Fund.

What is the asset allocation of SBI Equity Hybrid Fund?
The fund maintains 65–80% in equity and 20–35% in debt and money market instruments as per SEBI's aggressive hybrid fund classification.

What is the benchmark for SBI Equity Hybrid Fund?
The scheme benchmark is CRISIL Hybrid 35+65 Aggressive Index.

How is SBI Equity Hybrid Fund different from a pure equity fund?
Unlike pure equity funds, SBI Equity Hybrid Fund has a mandatory debt allocation (20–35%) which provides some stability during market downturns while still participating in equity market growth.
