About SBI Focused Equity Fund:
SBI Focused Equity Fund is a focused equity mutual fund from SBI Mutual Fund that invests in a concentrated portfolio of maximum 30 high-conviction stocks across market capitalisations (large, mid, and small cap). The fund uses a bottom-up investment approach for stock selection.

Fund Manager: R. Srinivasan (since October 2012)
