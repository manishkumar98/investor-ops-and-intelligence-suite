About SBI Contra Fund:
SBI Contra Fund is a contra equity mutual fund from SBI Mutual Fund that follows a contrarian investment strategy. It invests in stocks that are currently out of favour with the market but have strong fundamentals. The fund is benchmarked against the BSE 500 TRI and is one of the best-performing contra funds in India over the long term.

Fund Manager: Dinesh Balachandran (since October 2018)

What does contrarian investing mean in SBI Contra Fund?
The fund invests in stocks that are temporarily out of favour with the market — where the market has overreacted negatively to news — but the underlying business fundamentals remain strong. This approach aims to buy undervalued stocks before the market recognises their true value.
