About:
SBI Technology Opportunities Fund is a sectoral equity fund that invests predominantly in technology, media, and telecom companies. It captures growth from India's booming IT services sector, digital transformation themes, and global technology outsourcing. The fund invests in both large-cap IT bellwethers (TCS, Infosys, Wipro) and emerging mid/small-cap technology companies. Sectoral funds carry higher concentration risk and are suited for investors who want focused technology exposure within a diversified portfolio.

Is technology sector investing risky?
Yes. Sectoral funds are among the riskiest mutual fund categories as they concentrate in one sector. Technology stocks can be impacted by rupee appreciation, US discretionary IT spending slowdowns, and global macro factors. Investors should limit sectoral allocation to 10–15% of overall portfolio.

How does the fund benefit from India's IT sector?
India's IT sector exports over $250 billion annually. Companies like TCS, Infosys, and Wipro generate most revenue from US and European clients. The fund benefits from IT sector growth, margins, and any tailwinds from global digital transformation.
