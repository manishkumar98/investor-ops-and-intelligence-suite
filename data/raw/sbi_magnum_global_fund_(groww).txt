About:
SBI Magnum Global Fund is one of India's oldest and most respected MNC-themed equity mutual funds, running since 1994. It invests a minimum of 80% in equity securities of Multi-National Companies (MNCs) — Indian-listed companies where a foreign parent holds a significant stake (typically 26%+). MNCs often offer superior corporate governance, access to global R&D and technology, stable dividend payouts, and lower promoter risk compared to Indian-promoter-controlled companies. The fund does NOT invest in foreign stocks directly — all holdings are Indian-listed securities of MNC subsidiaries.

What is an MNC fund?
An MNC fund invests in Indian-listed companies that have significant foreign multinational parentage. Examples: Hindustan Unilever (parent: Unilever UK), Nestle India (parent: Nestle Switzerland), ABB India (parent: ABB Switzerland).

Does SBI Magnum Global Fund invest in US or global stocks?
No. The fund only invests in Indian-listed securities of MNC subsidiaries. It does not directly hold foreign stocks or international ETFs.

What are the advantages of MNC companies?
- Better corporate governance and global best practices
- Access to parent company's technology, brands, and R&D
- Regular dividends — MNC parents often mandate dividend payouts
- Lower promoter-related fraud risk
- Relatively stable earnings growth
