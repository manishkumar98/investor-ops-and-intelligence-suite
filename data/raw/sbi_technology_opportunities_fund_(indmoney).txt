About SBI Technology Opportunities Fund:
SBI Technology Opportunities Fund is a sectoral equity mutual fund from SBI Mutual Fund that invests predominantly in technology and IT-related companies. The fund is designed for investors seeking focused exposure to India's fast-growing technology sector. It is benchmarked against the BSE Teck Index TRI.

Fund Manager: Saurabh Pant (since August 2021)

Frequently Asked Questions:
What is the exit load of SBI Technology Opportunities Fund?
The exit load is 1% if redeemed within 15 days. No exit load after 15 days.

What is the expense ratio?
The expense ratio is 1.14% (Direct Plan).

What is the AUM of the fund?
The AUM is ₹4,892 Cr.

What is the minimum SIP?
The minimum SIP is ₹500 per month.

Is SBI Technology Opportunities Fund suitable for all investors?
No. This is a high-risk sectoral fund suitable only for investors who understand the technology sector and can accept high concentration risk and short-term volatility.
