About:
SBI Focused Equity Fund is an open-ended equity scheme investing in maximum 30 stocks across market caps. The concentrated portfolio allows deeper conviction bets while maintaining diversification across at least 3 sectors. The fund follows a bottom-up stock selection approach with long-term horizon.

FAQs:
What is the exit load for SBI Focused Equity Fund?
1% if redeemed within 1 year from date of allotment. Nil after 1 year.

What is the expense ratio of SBI Focused Equity Fund?
0.84% for Direct Plan. Regular Plan has higher expense ratio.

Why is it called a Focused Fund?
SEBI limits focused funds to a maximum of 30 stocks. This concentration can amplify returns when picks are right, but also increases fund-specific risk compared to diversified equity funds.
