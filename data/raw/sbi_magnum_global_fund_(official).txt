Fund Overview:
SBI Magnum Global Fund is an open-ended thematic equity scheme that aims to provide investors with opportunities for long-term growth in capital by investing predominantly in equity and equity-related securities of Multi-National Companies (MNCs) listed in India.

The fund invests a minimum of 80% of its assets in MNC stocks — companies where the foreign parent/promoter holds a significant stake (typically 26% or more), providing exposure to global management practices, technology transfer, and stable governance.

MNCs typically offer:
- Strong corporate governance and global best practices
- Access to parent company's technology and R&D
- Stable dividend payouts
- Relatively lower volatility compared to purely domestic companies

FAQ Section:
What is SBI Magnum Global Fund?
SBI Magnum Global Fund is a thematic equity mutual fund that invests in Multi-National Companies (MNCs) listed in India. MNCs are companies where a foreign parent or promoter holds a significant stake, offering exposure to global management quality and stable governance.

What is the exit load of SBI Magnum Global Fund?
The exit load is 1% if units are redeemed within 1 year from the date of allotment. There is no exit load after 1 year.

What is the expense ratio of SBI Magnum Global Fund?
The expense ratio for the Direct Plan is 0.98% and for the Regular Plan is 1.92%.

What is the minimum SIP amount?
The minimum SIP amount is ₹500.

Is there a lock-in period?
No, there is no mandatory lock-in period for SBI Magnum Global Fund.

What is the benchmark for SBI Magnum Global Fund?
The scheme benchmark is BSE MNC Index TRI.

What types of companies does SBI Magnum Global Fund invest in?
The fund invests in Indian-listed companies where a foreign multinational parent holds a significant stake, such as Hindustan Unilever, Nestle India, ABB India, Honeywell Automation, Siemens, and similar MNC subsidiaries.
