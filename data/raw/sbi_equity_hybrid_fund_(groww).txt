About:
SBI Equity Hybrid Fund is one of India's largest aggressive hybrid mutual funds, with over ₹68,000 Cr AUM. As per SEBI classification, aggressive hybrid funds invest 65–80% in equity and 20–35% in debt. The equity component aims for long-term capital appreciation while the debt portion (government securities, AAA corporate bonds, money market instruments) provides stability and cushions against sharp equity market drawdowns. The fund is suitable for first-time equity investors and those wanting equity growth with some downside protection.

What is the exit load for SBI Equity Hybrid Fund?

What is the equity-debt allocation?
65–80% in equity, 20–35% in debt and money market instruments as per SEBI's aggressive hybrid fund rules. Current allocation: ~72% equity, ~23% debt.

Is SBI Equity Hybrid Fund good for first-time investors?
Yes. The debt component (20–35%) reduces overall portfolio volatility compared to pure equity funds, making it a gentler introduction to equity markets for new investors.

How is SBI Equity Hybrid Fund taxed?
Since equity allocation is ≥65%, it is taxed like an equity fund: LTCG at 10% (above ₹1L) for > 1 year holding; STCG at 15% for < 1 year.
