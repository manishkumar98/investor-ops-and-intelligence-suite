Fund Overview:
SBI Liquid Fund is an open-ended liquid scheme that aims to provide investors with regular income and high liquidity by investing in money market instruments and short-term debt securities with a residual maturity of up to 91 days.

The fund invests in a portfolio of money market instruments, treasury bills, commercial papers, certificates of deposit, and other short-term debt instruments.

The fund is suitable for parking surplus funds for short periods (1 day to 3 months) and for emergency fund management.

FAQ Section:
What is SBI Liquid Fund?
SBI Liquid Fund is an open-ended debt mutual fund that invests in money market instruments and short-term debt securities with residual maturity up to 91 days. It is ideal for parking surplus cash for very short periods with high liquidity and low risk.

What is the exit load of SBI Liquid Fund?
SBI Liquid Fund has a graded exit load for redemptions within the first 6 days:
Day 1: 0.0070%, Day 2: 0.0065%, Day 3: 0.0060%, Day 4: 0.0055%, Day 5: 0.0050%, Day 6: 0.0045%. From Day 7 onwards there is no exit load.

What is the expense ratio of SBI Liquid Fund?
The expense ratio for the Direct Plan is 0.20% and for the Regular Plan is 0.33%.

What is the minimum investment in SBI Liquid Fund?
The minimum lumpsum investment is ₹5,000 and the minimum SIP is ₹1,000.

Is SBI Liquid Fund safe?
SBI Liquid Fund invests in high-quality money market instruments. The riskometer is Low to Moderate. However, it is not risk-free and returns may vary.

Is there a lock-in period for SBI Liquid Fund?
No, there is no mandatory lock-in period. However, a graded exit load applies for redemptions within the first 6 days.

What is the benchmark for SBI Liquid Fund?
The scheme benchmark is CRISIL Liquid Fund AI Index.
