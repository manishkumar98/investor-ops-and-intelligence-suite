About:
SBI Healthcare Opportunities Fund is a sectoral equity fund investing predominantly in pharmaceutical, healthcare services, diagnostics, hospitals, and medical devices companies. India is the world's pharmacy — a leading exporter of generic medicines to the US, Europe, and emerging markets. The fund captures this long-term structural theme as well as growing domestic healthcare consumption. Healthcare is considered a relatively defensive sector but still carries volatility from US FDA approvals/rejections, pricing pressure, and regulatory changes.

Why does healthcare tend to be defensive?
Demand for medicines, hospitals, and diagnostics is relatively inelastic — people need healthcare regardless of economic cycles. This makes healthcare stocks relatively resilient during market downturns compared to cyclical sectors.

What risks are specific to pharma/healthcare funds?
Key risks include: US FDA import alerts on manufacturing facilities, drug pricing pressure in export markets, NPPA price controls on domestic drugs, and clinical trial outcomes. These can cause sharp stock-specific corrections.
