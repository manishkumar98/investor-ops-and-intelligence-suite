Fund Overview:
SBI Technology Opportunities Fund is an open-ended sectoral equity scheme that aims to provide investors with opportunities for long-term growth in capital by investing predominantly in equity and equity-related securities of technology and technology-related companies.

The fund invests a minimum of 80% of its assets in equity and equity-related securities of technology sector companies including IT services, software, hardware, semiconductors, telecom, and internet companies.

The fund follows a bottom-up approach to stock selection within the technology sector.

Important Note:
Sectoral funds like SBI Technology Opportunities Fund invest in a single sector, making them more volatile than diversified equity funds. These are suitable only for investors who understand the technology sector and can accept high concentration risk.

FAQ Section:
What is SBI Technology Opportunities Fund?
SBI Technology Opportunities Fund is a sectoral equity mutual fund that invests predominantly in technology and IT-related companies. It is suitable for investors with a high risk appetite who want focused exposure to the technology sector.

What is the exit load of SBI Technology Opportunities Fund?
The exit load is 1% if units are redeemed within 15 days from the date of allotment. There is no exit load after 15 days.

What is the expense ratio of SBI Technology Opportunities Fund?
The expense ratio for the Direct Plan is 1.14% and for the Regular Plan is 2.10%.

What is the minimum SIP amount?
The minimum SIP amount is ₹500.

Is there a lock-in period?
No, there is no mandatory lock-in period.

What is the benchmark for SBI Technology Opportunities Fund?
The scheme benchmark is BSE Teck Index TRI.

What sectors does SBI Technology Opportunities Fund invest in?
The fund invests in IT services, software products, hardware, semiconductors, telecom, internet companies, and other technology-related sectors.
