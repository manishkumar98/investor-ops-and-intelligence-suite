About SBI ELSS Tax Saver Fund:
SBI ELSS Tax Saver Fund (formerly SBI Long Term Equity Fund) is one of India's oldest and largest Equity Linked Savings Schemes. It invests predominantly in equity and equity-related securities across market caps and qualifies for tax deduction under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act, up to ₹1,50,000 per year. The mandatory 3-year lock-in period aligns with long-term wealth creation goals.

Fund Manager: Dinesh Balachandran (since October 2018)

Frequently Asked Questions:
What is the lock-in period for SBI ELSS Tax Saver Fund?
There is a mandatory 3-year lock-in from the date of each investment/SIP instalment. Units cannot be redeemed before completion of 3 years.

What is the tax benefit of SBI ELSS Tax Saver Fund?
Investments up to ₹1,50,000 per financial year qualify for deduction under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act, potentially saving up to ₹46,800 in tax (for 30% tax bracket).

What is the exit load?
There is no exit load for SBI ELSS Tax Saver Fund. The 3-year lock-in serves as the holding requirement.

What is the expense ratio?
The expense ratio is 0.99% (Direct Plan).

What is the AUM?
The AUM is ₹25,612 Cr.

What is the minimum SIP?
The minimum SIP is ₹500 per month. Each SIP instalment has its own 3-year lock-in.

What are the tax implications on redemption?
Gains are treated as Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG). Gains up to ₹1 lakh per year are tax-free; gains above ₹1 lakh are taxed at 10% without indexation benefit.
