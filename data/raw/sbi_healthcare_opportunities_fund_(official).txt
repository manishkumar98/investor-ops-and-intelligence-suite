Fund Overview:
SBI Healthcare Opportunities Fund is an open-ended sectoral equity scheme that aims to provide investors with opportunities for long-term growth by investing predominantly in equity and equity-related securities of companies engaged in healthcare, pharmaceuticals, hospitals, diagnostics, and allied healthcare businesses.

The fund invests a minimum of 80% of its assets in healthcare and pharma-related companies including domestic pharma, API manufacturers, hospitals, diagnostics chains, and healthcare services companies.

FAQ Section:
What is SBI Healthcare Opportunities Fund?
SBI Healthcare Opportunities Fund is a sectoral equity mutual fund that invests predominantly in healthcare and pharmaceutical companies including hospitals, diagnostics, API manufacturers, and health services companies.

What is the exit load of SBI Healthcare Opportunities Fund?
The exit load is 1% if units are redeemed within 15 days from the date of allotment. There is no exit load after 15 days.

What is the expense ratio of SBI Healthcare Opportunities Fund?
The expense ratio for the Direct Plan is 1.18% and for the Regular Plan is 2.14%.

What is the minimum SIP amount?
The minimum SIP amount is ₹500.

Is there a lock-in period?
No, there is no mandatory lock-in period.

What is the benchmark for SBI Healthcare Opportunities Fund?
The scheme benchmark is S&P BSE Healthcare Index TRI.

What types of companies does SBI Healthcare Opportunities Fund invest in?
The fund invests in pharmaceutical companies, hospitals and healthcare service providers, diagnostic chains, API (Active Pharmaceutical Ingredient) manufacturers, and medical devices companies.
