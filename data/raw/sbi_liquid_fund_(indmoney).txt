About SBI Liquid Fund:
SBI Liquid Fund is a liquid mutual fund from SBI Mutual Fund that invests in money market instruments and debt securities with a residual maturity of up to 91 days. It is designed for investors who want to park surplus cash with easy liquidity and low risk. The fund is one of the largest liquid funds in India by AUM.

Fund Manager: R. Arun (since January 2020)
