About SBI Equity Hybrid Fund:
SBI Equity Hybrid Fund is one of India's largest aggressive hybrid mutual funds from SBI Mutual Fund. It invests 65–80% in equities across market capitalisations and 20–35% in high-quality debt instruments. The fund is suitable for investors seeking equity-led growth with moderate downside protection from the debt component.

Fund Managers: R. Srinivasan (Equity, since Jan 2013), Dinesh Ahuja (Debt, since Jan 2014)
