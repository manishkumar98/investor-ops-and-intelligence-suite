Fund Overview:
SBI Contra Fund is an open-ended equity scheme that follows a contrarian investment strategy. The fund aims to provide investors with long-term capital appreciation by investing in stocks that are currently out of favour with the market but have strong fundamentals and are expected to recover over time.

The fund invests in undervalued and out-of-favour stocks across market capitalisations with a contrarian approach, looking for opportunities where market sentiment differs significantly from business fundamentals.

The fund uses a value-oriented, bottom-up approach to stock selection, focusing on companies that are temporarily depressed in price but have strong long-term potential.

FAQ Section:
What is SBI Contra Fund?
SBI Contra Fund is an open-ended equity scheme that follows a contrarian investment strategy. It invests in stocks that are currently out of favour with the market but have strong business fundamentals. The fund is suitable for investors with a long investment horizon who can tolerate high risk.

What is the exit load of SBI Contra Fund?
The exit load is 1% if units are redeemed within 1 year from the date of allotment. There is no exit load after 1 year.

What is the expense ratio of SBI Contra Fund?
The expense ratio for the Direct Plan is 0.62% and for the Regular Plan is 1.58%.

What is the minimum SIP amount for SBI Contra Fund?
The minimum SIP amount is ₹500.

Is there a lock-in period for SBI Contra Fund?
No, there is no mandatory lock-in period for SBI Contra Fund.

What is the benchmark for SBI Contra Fund?
The scheme benchmark is BSE 500 TRI and the additional benchmark is BSE Sensex TRI.

What is a contrarian investment strategy?
A contrarian investment strategy involves investing in stocks or sectors that are currently out of favour with the broader market. The idea is that the market sometimes overreacts to negative news, creating buying opportunities in fundamentally strong companies whose prices are temporarily depressed.
