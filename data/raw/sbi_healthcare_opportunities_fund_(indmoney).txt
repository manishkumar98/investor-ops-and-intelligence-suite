About SBI Healthcare Opportunities Fund:
SBI Healthcare Opportunities Fund is a sectoral equity mutual fund from SBI Mutual Fund that invests in healthcare and pharmaceutical companies. The fund captures growth from India's expanding healthcare sector including pharma exports, domestic healthcare consumption, and hospital infrastructure.

Fund Manager: Tanmaya Desai (since June 2011)

What is the exit load of SBI Healthcare Opportunities Fund?

Is SBI Healthcare Opportunities Fund suitable for all investors?
No. This is a high-risk sectoral fund. It is suitable for investors with a long investment horizon who understand the pharma and healthcare sector and can tolerate concentration risk and regulatory/policy-driven volatility.
