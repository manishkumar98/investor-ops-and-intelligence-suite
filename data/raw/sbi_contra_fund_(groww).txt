About:
SBI Contra Fund follows a contrarian investment philosophy — it invests in fundamentally strong companies that are currently out-of-favour, undervalued, or overlooked by the broader market. The fund bets on a mean reversion thesis: that quality businesses trading at a discount will eventually recover. This approach requires patience (typically 3–5 year horizon) and may underperform during momentum-driven bull markets, but can significantly outperform during recoveries.

What is the exit load for SBI Contra Fund?

What is the expense ratio?

What does "contra" or "contrarian" investing mean?
Contrarian investing means buying stocks that the broader market has ignored or sold off, betting they are undervalued. SBI Contra Fund seeks to buy quality businesses at beaten-down prices and hold until the market recognises their value.

Is SBI Contra Fund suitable for short-term investors?
No. Contrarian strategies require patience — value unlocking can take 3–5 years. The fund is suitable for investors with a long-term horizon (5+ years) and ability to tolerate periods of underperformance.
