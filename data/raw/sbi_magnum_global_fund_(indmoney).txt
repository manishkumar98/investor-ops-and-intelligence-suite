About SBI Magnum Global Fund:
SBI Magnum Global Fund is a thematic equity mutual fund from SBI Mutual Fund that invests in Multi-National Companies (MNCs) listed in India. These are companies where a foreign parent holds a significant stake, providing exposure to global management quality, technology transfers, and strong governance standards. The fund is one of the oldest MNC-themed funds in India.

Fund Manager: Richard D'souza (since April 2014)

What makes SBI Magnum Global Fund different from other equity funds?
The fund exclusively invests in MNC subsidiaries listed in India, which typically offer more stable governance, lower promoter risk, access to global technology, and often regular dividends compared to purely domestic companies.

Does SBI Magnum Global Fund invest in foreign stocks?
No. The fund invests only in Indian-listed securities of companies that have significant foreign multinational parentage. It does not directly invest in overseas stocks.
