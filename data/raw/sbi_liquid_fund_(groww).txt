About:
SBI Liquid Fund is one of India's largest liquid funds by AUM. It invests in short-term debt and money market instruments with maturity up to 91 days — including Treasury Bills, Commercial Papers, Certificates of Deposit, and Repos. It is designed for parking surplus cash for the short term with minimal risk and same/next day liquidity.

FAQs:
What is the exit load for SBI Liquid Fund?
Graded exit load applies for the first 7 days (Day 1: 0.0070% to Day 6: 0.0045%). No exit load after Day 7.

What is the expense ratio?
0.20% for Direct Plan — among the lowest in the category due to short-term, low-cost instruments.

Is SBI Liquid Fund safe?
Liquid funds invest only in high-quality debt instruments with up to 91-day maturity. Credit risk and interest rate risk are minimal. However, they are not capital-guaranteed like bank FDs.

How is SBI Liquid Fund taxed?
Liquid fund gains are taxed as per your income tax slab (both short-term and long-term, as per the Finance Act 2023 amendments). TDS may apply on gains.

When are redemption proceeds credited?
Redemptions are processed same day (if submitted before cut-off) or next business day. IMPS/NEFT credit typically within a few hours to 1 business day.
