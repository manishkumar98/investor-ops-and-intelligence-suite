About SBI Large Cap Fund:
SBI Large Cap Fund (formerly SBI Bluechip Fund) is an open-ended large cap equity mutual fund from SBI Mutual Fund that invests predominantly in equity and equity-related securities of large cap companies. Large cap companies are ranked 1–100 by market capitalisation. The fund follows a growth-oriented investment style with a focus on quality large-cap businesses.

Fund Manager: Sohini Andani (since June 2010)

Frequently Asked Questions:
What is the exit load of SBI Large Cap Fund?
The exit load is 1% if redeemed within 1 year. No exit load after 1 year.

What is the expense ratio?

What is the AUM?

What is the minimum SIP?

What type of stocks does SBI Large Cap Fund invest in?
The fund invests in top 100 companies by market cap (large caps) listed on Indian stock exchanges. These companies are typically industry leaders with established business models and stable earnings.

Is SBI Large Cap Fund suitable for conservative equity investors?
Yes. Large cap funds are considered less volatile than mid and small cap funds, making SBI Large Cap Fund suitable for investors with moderate risk appetite seeking long-term capital appreciation.
