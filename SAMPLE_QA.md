## Factual Queries — Fund Facts (M1 corpus)

### Q1. What is the expense ratio of SBI Large Cap Fund Direct plan?

**Answer:**  
The expense ratio of SBI Large Cap Fund (Direct plan) is **0.84% per annum**. This is the Total Expense Ratio (TER) charged annually as a percentage of AUM — it is already deducted from the NAV and does not appear as a separate line item on your statement.

- Benchmark: BSE 100 TRI
- Minimum SIP: ₹500/month

**Source:** https://www.sbimf.com/sbimf-scheme-details/sbi-large-cap-fund-(formerly-known-as-sbi-bluechip-fund)-43  
Last updated from sources: 2026-04-29

### Q2. What is the lock-in period for SBI ELSS Tax Saver Fund?

**Answer:**  
SBI ELSS Tax Saver Fund has a **mandatory 3-year lock-in period** per unit. Each SIP instalment is locked in for 3 years from its respective investment date. Redemption before 3 years is not permitted — there is no exit load (0%) because the lock-in period itself acts as the hold constraint.

- Tax benefit: Deduction under Section 80C (up to ₹1.5 lakh per year)
- Minimum SIP: ₹500/month

**Source:** https://www.sbimf.com/sbimf-scheme-details/SBI-ELSS-Tax-Saver-Fund-(formerly-known-as-SBI-Long-Term-Equity-Fund)-3  
Last updated from sources: 2026-04-29

### Q3. What is the exit load for SBI Small Cap Fund?

**Answer:**  
SBI Small Cap Fund charges an exit load of **1% if units are redeemed within 12 months** of purchase. No exit load applies after 12 months. The expense ratio (Direct plan) is **0.79% p.a.**

- Benchmark: BSE 250 SmallCap Index TRI
- Minimum SIP: ₹500/month

**Source:** https://www.sbimf.com/sbimf-scheme-details/SBI-Small-Cap-Fund-329  
Last updated from sources: 2026-04-29

### Q4. How do I download my capital gains statement for SBI Mutual Fund?

**Answer:**  
You can download your capital gains statement from **CAMS Online** — the registrar and transfer agent for SBI Mutual Fund:

1. Visit https://www.camsonline.com/Investors/Statements/Capital-Gains-Statement
2. Select "Capital Gains Statement" and enter your registered email/PAN
3. Choose the financial year and fund house (SBI Mutual Fund)
4. Statement is emailed to your registered address

You can also access it via the INDMoney app under **Portfolio → Statements → Capital Gains**.

**Source:** https://www.camsonline.com/Investors/Statements/Capital-Gains-Statement  
Last updated from sources: 2026-04-29

## Compound Queries — Fund Facts + Fee Scenario (M1 + M2 corpus)

### Q5. What is the exit load for SBI ELSS and why was I charged it?

**Answer:**  
SBI ELSS Tax Saver Fund has **0% exit load** — you cannot be charged an exit load on ELSS redemption. The 3-year lock-in period serves as the hold constraint instead. If a deduction appeared on your statement it is likely one of the following:

- **Expense ratio (0.99% p.a.):** Deducted daily from NAV; shows as a lower NAV value, not a line-item charge
- **STT (Securities Transaction Tax):** Levied by the government on equity redemptions at 0.001%
- **Capital gains tax:** Applicable on gains at redemption (LTCG at 12.5% above ₹1.25 lakh per year)

For a detailed fee breakdown, check your CAS (Consolidated Account Statement) via CAMS or MFCentral.

**Sources:**  
- https://www.sbimf.com/sbimf-scheme-details/SBI-ELSS-Tax-Saver-Fund-(formerly-known-as-SBI-Long-Term-Equity-Fund)-3  
- https://www.sbimf.com/en-us/investor-service/exit-load  
Last updated from sources: 2026-04-29

### Q6. What is the minimum SIP for SBI ELSS and what fee components apply?

**Answer:**  
The minimum SIP for SBI ELSS Tax Saver Fund is **₹500/month**. The following fee components apply:

- **Expense Ratio:** 0.99% p.a. (Direct plan) — deducted from NAV daily
- **Exit Load:** 0% — no redemption charge (lock-in substitutes this)
- **Entry Load:** Nil — abolished by SEBI for all mutual funds since 2009
- **STT on redemption:** 0.001% of redemption amount (government levy)
- **LTCG tax:** 12.5% on gains exceeding ₹1.25 lakh per financial year
- **Last checked:** 2026-04-29
